Our Story

Deeply human

Robyn was founded by Dr. Jenny Shao, a Harvard-trained physician and surgeon with 7 years of experience in neuroscience research, including 3 years of research on the biological basis of memory with Nobel Laureate Eric Kandel, which served as the ground work for Robyn's relational memory architecture.

Jenny left her lifelong career in medicine after witnessing a deeper epidemic in her patients — disconnection. She built Robyn to be the world's first relational AI to bridge this gap and empower the humans of the world to be truly seen and known.

Her and her team blend neuroscience, AI, and design to build technology that makes people more human, not less.
